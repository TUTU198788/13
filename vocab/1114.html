28整句提示 1/3
虽然路上车很多，但我还是准时到校。
Although there is a lot of traffic on the road, I arrive at school on time.
🚗 Although…, 不要 but
Although there is a lot of traffic on the road, I arrive at school on time.
  • although / though 不和 but 连用，只能二选一。
  • traffic 不可数，所以是 a lot of traffic，谓语用 is。
  • arrive at + 小地点 = get to；“到校”是 at school，不加 the。
  • on time 准时；in time 是“及时”。笔记里写的 get to / arrive at 都对，这里统一用 arrive at。
